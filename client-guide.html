Overview

How the System Works

When someone submits an order on your website, two things happen automatically: you get a text message with a summary, and the order is added to your Square Appointments calendar. You don't need to do anything — it's all automatic.

🌐 Customer fills out the order form on your website
↓
📱 You get an SMS on your phone with a summary
↓
📅 Booking appears in your Square Appointments calendar
↓
👤 Customer record saved in Square Customers
💡
Good to know

You only need to check your phone for the SMS. Everything else lives in Square, which you can open on your phone or computer any time.

Section 1

The Text Message (SMS) You Receive

Every time a new order comes in, your phone will receive a text message that looks like this:

NEW ORDER - Ony's Boutique From: Jane Smith (587-555-1234) Date: 2026-07-15 | Pickup Occasion: Birthday Cake: Chocolate, Buttercream, 24 servings Add-ons: freshFlorals, disco

2:14 PM  ✓

From Customer name and phone number — tap to call or text them back
Date The pickup or delivery date they chose on the form
Occasion What the cake is for (Birthday, Wedding, Corporate, etc.)
Cake Flavor, frosting, and how many servings they need
Add-ons Any extras they selected (florals, disco balls, etc.)
💡
Tip

You don't need to reply to the SMS. It's just a heads-up. The full details — including the customer's email — are in your Square calendar.

Section 2

Finding Your Orders in Square

Your orders live in Square Appointments. Here's how to get there:

1 Open Square

On your phone or computer, open the Square app or go to squareup.com and sign in to your Ony's Boutique Cakes account.

2 Go to Appointments

Tap or click Appointments in the left-hand menu.

3 Find the date

Use the calendar at the top to navigate to the date the customer chose for pickup or delivery. New orders appear as coloured appointment blocks on that date.

💡
Tip about the time

All orders are stored at 12:00 PM on the pickup/delivery date. This is just how they're saved — it doesn't mean the customer is coming at noon. The actual time is something you agree with the customer separately.

Section 4

Managing Your Calendar

a Day, Week, and Month views

Switch between Day, Week, and Month view using the buttons at the top right of the Appointments screen. Week view is usually the most useful — you can see all upcoming orders at a glance.

b A busy week

During busy periods you'll see multiple bookings stacked across different dates. Each block shows the customer's name so you know who it's for at a glance.

c Colours and status

Square shows bookings in different colours based on their status:

  • Upcoming bookings — shown in your brand colour (blue or the colour you set in Square)
  • Past bookings — shown in grey once the date has passed
  • Cancelled bookings — may be hidden or shown with a strikethrough
Section 5

Your Customer List

a Where to find it

Every customer who orders through the website is automatically saved in Square Customers. You can find it in the left menu under Customers.

b What's saved for each customer

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Their booking history (all orders they've ever placed)

This list builds up over time — after a few months you'll have a full record of everyone who has ever ordered from you.

c Finding a specific customer

Use the search bar at the top of the Customers page to find someone by name, email, or phone number.

Section 6

Cancelling or Rescheduling a Booking

a Cancelling a booking

If a customer calls to cancel their order:

  1. Open Appointments in Square
  2. Navigate to their booking date and click the appointment block
  3. Click the three dots (…) menu in the top right of the appointment panel
  4. Select Cancel appointment
  5. Confirm when Square asks you
⚠️
Important

Cancelling in Square does NOT automatically notify the customer. You should call or text them separately to confirm the cancellation.

b Rescheduling to a new date

If a customer wants to change their pickup or delivery date:

  1. Open the existing appointment in Square Appointments
  2. Click Edit
  3. Change the date to the new date
  4. Click Save
💡
Alternative

If changing the date is complicated, you can also cancel the old booking and ask the customer to submit a new order through the website with the correct date. Either approach works fine.

Section 7

Troubleshooting

If something doesn't look right, check the table below first:

Problem What to do
I submitted an order but didn't get an SMS Check your phone signal. If signal is fine, contact your developer — the Twilio number may need to be checked.
The booking isn't showing on my calendar Check the date — bookings always appear at 12:00 PM on the pickup/delivery date. Try switching to Week or Month view to make it easier to spot.
I see a booking but the notes are empty This shouldn't happen. Contact your developer and give them the booking ID (visible in the appointment URL in your browser).
A customer submitted the form twice by accident Open Square Appointments, find both bookings for that customer, keep one, and cancel the other using the steps in Section 6.
I can't find the Appointments section in Square Make sure you're logged into the correct Square account — the one for Ony's Boutique Cakes. Ask your developer for the login details if needed.
The website form isn't working / customers say they can't submit Contact your developer. The Square or Twilio service may be temporarily down. In the meantime, take orders by phone.
📞
Need help?

Contact your developer with as much detail as possible: what happened, when, and any error messages you saw. A screenshot always helps!
